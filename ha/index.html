1 · Saka

  1. Windows: buɗe fayil ɗin .exe. Idan Windows ya nuna gargaɗi: Ƙarin bayani › Gudanar duk da haka.
  2. Mac: buɗe .dmg, ja manhajar cikin Applications, sannan a farkon buɗewa: danna dama › Buɗe.
  3. Harshe: zaɓi Français, English ko Hausa a saman allon kunnawa (ko a Saituna).

2 · Gwada sannan ka kunna

  1. Danna Gwada … na kwanaki 14 (START ga manhajojin fanni ɗaya, GESTION ga TAMTUI SAHEL 360).
  2. Don cikakkiyar siga: Kwafi lambar kwamfutar ka aika wa mai ba ka TAMTUI.
  3. Shigo da fayil ɗin lasisin da ka karɓa: manhajar za ta kunna har abada.

  • Windows 10 ko 11 (64 bits) — ba a tallafa wa Windows 7 da 8 ba

Farashin TAMTUI SAHEL 360 shi ne na manhajojin daban-daban + 15 %: ayyukanka suna haɗe a tushe ɗaya (alaƙa tsakanin ayyuka, Bita 360°, taswira da rahotanni na tare). Don aiki ɗaya kawai, zaɓi manhajar fanni ɗaya.

  • Ƙarin kwamfuta: ragi −20 % a farashin lasisi.
  • Horo (ba dole ba, ana ba da shawara): sau ɗaya, 30 % na farashin lasisi.
  • Canza kwamfuta (lalacewa, sata, maye gurbi): sabon lasisi a 40 % na farashi.
  • Ƙarin aiki ko siga daga baya: za ka biya bambancin kawai, bayananka suna nan.
  • Kana amfani da manhajar TAMTUI tuni? Don komawa TAMTUI SAHEL 360, za a cire kuɗin lasisinka kuma a ɗauko bayananka.
  • Ayyuka, ƙungiyoyi, hukumomin gwamnati: lasisin aiki, na yanki ko na ƙasa bisa ƙiyasin farashi.

🔌 Kwamfutar Windows ba tare da intanet ba?

Windows 11 da Windows 10 da aka sabunta suna shirye. A kan Windows 10 da ba a taɓa sabuntawa ba kuma babu intanet, sakawa tana buƙatar wani abu na Microsoft (WebView2): kawo shi tare da manhajar a filasha.

  1. Inda akwai intanet, sauke mai saka manhajar na Windows da abin da ke gefe, sannan ka kwafe su biyun a filasha.
  2. A kan kwamfutar da babu intanet, da farko ka buɗe abin na Microsoft (sau ɗaya ga kowace kwamfuta, don dukan manhajojin TAMTUI).
  3. Sannan ka buɗe mai saka TAMTUI: zai saku ba tare da intanet ba.
